# Quran Circle - Brand Guide

## App Name
- **English**: Quran Circle
- **Arabic**: حلقة القرآن (Halaqat al-Quran)

## Language Detection

The app automatically detects the user's preferred language using:

1. **Browser Language Detection**: Checks `navigator.language` to detect device/browser language
2. **Arabic Auto-Detection**: If browser language starts with 'ar' (e.g., ar-SA, ar-EG, ar-AE), defaults to Arabic
3. **User Preference Priority**: Once user manually selects a language, that choice is saved and takes priority
4. **Fallback**: Defaults to English for all other languages

**Supported Browser Locales for Arabic**:
- `ar` - Generic Arabic
- `ar-SA` - Saudi Arabia
- `ar-EG` - Egypt  
- `ar-AE` - UAE
- `ar-MA` - Morocco
- `ar-DZ` - Algeria
- And all other Arabic locale variants

## Brand Voice

### Tone Characteristics
- **Spiritual**: Emphasize the sacred nature of Quran engagement
- **Calm**: No urgency, no pressure, peaceful journey
- **Respectful**: Privacy-first, dignified approach
- **Supportive**: Community without social pressure
- **Focus-oriented**: Clarity over noise

### Key Messaging Pillars

1. **Privacy First**
   - No profiles, no usernames, no photos
   - Your journey is between you and Allah
   - Local storage for personal progress

2. **Zero Distractions**
   - No comments, no likes, no feeds
   - Just you and the Quran
   - Calm, focused environment

3. **Circle Community**
   - Join anonymous circles (halaqat)
   - Shared goals without social pressure
   - Inspired by traditional Islamic study circles

4. **Personal Progress**
   - Track reading and memorization privately
   - Streaks and milestones for motivation
   - Goal-oriented approach

## Terminology Guide

### Use "Circle" Language (Not "Group")

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use Instead |
|---------|---------------|
| Group | Circle |
| My Groups | My Circles |
| Join Group | Join Circle |
| Group Goals | Circle Goals |
| Group Members | Circle Members |
| In a group | In a circle |

### Arabic Translations

| English | Arabic |
|---------|--------|
| Circle | حلقة |
| Circles | الحلقات |
| My Circles | حلقاتي |
| Join Circle | انضم للحلقة |
| Circle Members | أعضاء الحلقة |
| Khatmah Circle | حلقة ختمة |
| Memorization Circle | حلقة حفظ |

### Circle Types

1. **Khatmah Circle** (حلقة ختمة)
   - For completing full Quran reading
   - Users can only join ONE at a time
   - Reading schedule-based

2. **Memorization Circle** (حلقة حفظ)
   - For memorizing specific surahs
   - Users can join MULTIPLE
   - Ayah-by-ayah tracking

3. **Study Circle** (حلقة دراسة)
   - For focused study (future feature)

## Value Propositions

### For Individuals
- Track reading progress privately
- Build memorization habits
- Complete personal khatmahs
- See streaks and milestones

### For Community
- Join circles for shared motivation
- Anonymous participation (no social pressure)
- Feel connected without distraction
- Traditional halaqah experience, modernized

## Cultural Context

The term "حلقة" (halaqah) has deep roots in Islamic tradition:
- Study circles in mosques where scholars taught students
- People sitting in a circle to learn Quran
- Emphasis on learning, not social validation
- Respectful, focused atmosphere

This app brings that tradition to the digital age while preserving its spiritual essence.
